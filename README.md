# aliya
ALIYA is your little space to write, create and remember ♡

Create aesthetic notes, diary entries, lists, study sessions and more — all in one soft, personal space designed to feel like your own digital stationery collection.

✨ Create notes, diaries and lists
🎀 Personalise pages with colours, fonts, icons and decorations
📅 Keep track of diary entries with a calendar
💗 Favourite and pin your favourite pages
✅ Make interactive to-do lists
📚 Track your study time and progress
📸 Add photos to your pages
↶ Undo and redo while writing
📤 Share your pages or export them as PDFs
☁️ Save and sync your pages across your account

Whether you're planning your week, studying, journalling, making a wishlist or simply getting your thoughts down, ALIYA gives you one little place to keep it all.

**ALIYA — your little space to write, create & remember ♡**
